The corresponding official LibRaw 0.22.2 Windows x64 package, including the complete source code and build files, accompanies this application as LibRaw-SOURCE.zip. A compatible modified libraw.dll may be placed beside PhotoReview.App.exe and loaded in place of the bundled library.
